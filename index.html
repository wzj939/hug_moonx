About LunarQuest

We are at the forefront of lunar exploration, developing innovative technologies and missions to study Earth's natural satellite.

Our Mission

LunarQuest is dedicated to advancing lunar exploration through cutting-edge technology, international collaboration, and scientific discovery. We aim to establish a sustainable human presence on the Moon by 2030.

Founded in 2015, our team of scientists, engineers, and astronauts has already completed three successful robotic missions to the lunar surface, gathering invaluable data about our cosmic neighbor.

Our research focuses on lunar geology, resource utilization, and preparing for future missions to Mars and beyond using the Moon as a stepping stone.
